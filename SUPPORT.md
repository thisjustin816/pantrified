# Pantrified Support

Pantrified is an early beta for managing household food and reducing waste.
Some screens and features are still being built. Check your TestFlight build's
What to Test notes for available features and known limitations.

## Report a problem or request a feature

Search the [existing issues](https://github.com/thisjustin816/pantrified/issues)
for your problem. If it has not been reported, [open an issue](https://github.com/thisjustin816/pantrified/issues/new).
Include:

- The app version and build number shown in TestFlight.
- Your iPhone model and iOS version.
- The steps that led to the problem, what you expected, and what happened.
- A screenshot if it helps, with personal information removed.

GitHub issues are public. Do not attach private household records, dietary or
health information, personal photos, passwords, or other sensitive information.
For feedback on a TestFlight build, you can also use Send Beta Feedback in
TestFlight.

## Common questions

**Why is a screen empty or a feature missing?**

The beta is under development. A feature described in the project's plans may
not be available in your installed build.

**Why is on-device generation unavailable in an evaluation build?**

It requires a compatible device with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model
ready. The app's minimum iOS version alone does not establish model availability.
The current runtime has no cloud fallback.

**Will deleting the app remove its data?**

Yes. Use caution before choosing Delete App in iOS. Offloading preserves app
data; deleting removes the local copy. Device backups are managed separately in
your Apple settings.
